# Persona — Maya Chen

**Primary user for TableTalk**

| | |
|---|---|
| **Age** | 24 |
| **Occupation** | Graduate student (part-time café barista) |
| **Location** | Oshawa / Toronto corridor |
| **Tech comfort** | High on phone & laptop; uses voice notes with friends, prefers typing in public |
| **Platform** | Mostly laptop at home or campus library (desktop-first matches her context) |

## Goals

- Reserve a restaurant table quickly for a study-group dinner or a weekend catch-up.
- Pick the right **date, time, party size, and seating** without hunting through long forms.
- Fix a wrong detail (e.g., “actually outdoor”) without restarting the whole booking.

## Needs & pain points

- Multi-step booking forms feel slow and easy to abandon mid-way.
- Voice is useful at home with headphones; typing is safer in a quiet library.
- Hates losing progress when an unavailable time slot appears late in the flow.
- Wants a clear summary before anything is “final.”

## Assumptions we design for

- She may give **several details in one sentence** (“tomorrow at 7 for 4 outdoor”).
- She may give **incomplete** answers (“Saturday”) and expect a natural follow-up.
- She may **mis-speak** or the mic may mis-hear; she needs a typed fallback.
- She will not provide payment or ID documents for a reservation prototype.

## Scenario (one evening)

Maya opens TableTalk on her laptop, meets Ava, answers a few prompts (or taps quick choices), edits the seating preference from the summary panel, confirms in the modal, and leaves with a confirmation code — under a couple of minutes.
